About STG

Technical capability and reliable execution for Qatar's process industries.

STG Industrial Services & Trading W.L.L is a Qatar-registered engineering and industrial supply company, built to serve the State of Qatar's process industries with technical capability and reliable execution.

Our founding team brings proven experience from the KSA process industries market. We specialise in the repair and overhaul of pumps, motors and mechanical seals — alongside single-source supply of steel, fittings, flanges, valves, gaskets and safety equipment.

“To earn long-term trust — one well-executed project at a time.”
