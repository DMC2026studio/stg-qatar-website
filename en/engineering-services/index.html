Mechanical seals

End-to-end seal capability

The mechanical seal is where reliability is won — or lost. STG offers repair, refurbishment, re-engineering and supply of new seals.

We are not tied to a single seal brand — we repair what you have, source what you need, and recommend what works best.

Repair

Worn seals restored to original performance.

Refurbishment

Faces, springs, secondary seals and metal components.

Re-engineering

Designs improved for life and reliability in difficult service.

Supply

New seals across all major OEM brands and designs.

Cartridge seals

Pre-assembled, factory-set units.

Component seals

Rubber bellows, pusher and metallic bellows.

Dual seals

Back-to-back, face-to-face and tandem.

Dry gas seals

Non-contacting, for compressors.

Split seals

Installed without dismantling equipment.

Agitator & mixer seals

Heavy-duty service with side loads and runout.

Machining

Precision machining of seal faces and metallic components.

Lapping

Lapping and polishing for seal-face flatness.

Testing

Pressure and leak testing of repaired or new assemblies.

Inspection

Dimensional inspection, material verification and failure analysis.
